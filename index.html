James Hagen Shorts Uploader

A private, single-user tool that reposts James Hagen's own short-form videos to his own YouTube channel.

What it does

James Hagen is a content creator who publishes short-form videos on TikTok. This tool takes videos he has already made and published there and uploads them to his YouTube channel, "James Hagen", as YouTube Shorts.

It runs twice a day (10:00 and 18:15 UK time) on his own home server and uploads at most two videos a day. If he has posted a new TikTok that isn't on YouTube yet, it uploads that one. If not, it uploads one of his older videos, starting with the ones that got the most engagement. Sponsored videos are skipped unless he is allowed to repost them, in which case they're uploaded with YouTube's paid promotion label.

Who can use it

Only James Hagen. The tool has no public sign-up and no other users. It only ever uploads to the one YouTube channel he owns, using the YouTube Data API with his permission.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How data is handled is set out in the privacy policy, which also links to the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this tool can be sent to jameshagen2008@gmail.com. The uploaded videos appear on the James Hagen YouTube channel.
